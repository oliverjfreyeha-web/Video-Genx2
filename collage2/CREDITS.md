# Credits — "First Reply Wins" (10 s collage spot #2 for Elevate Home Ads)

Photos come from the scikit-image sample-data catalogue (`skimage.data`); licences as stated there. They were cut out
with rembg, halftoned and given a rough white scissor edge by `collage2/make_assets.py` and `collage/make_assets.py`.

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Cat-headed homeowner: lid / face / jaw pieces (`cat_*.png`) | `skimage.data.chelsea` (photo by Stefan van der Walt) | CC0 |
| Coins (`coin1–5.png`) | `skimage.data.coins` — Greek coins from Pompeii, Brooklyn Museum collection | No known copyright restrictions |
| Deep space (`space.jpg`) | `skimage.data.hubble_deep_field` — Hubble Ultra Deep Field, NASA | Public domain (NASA) |
| Eagle (`eagle.png`) | `skimage.data.eagle` | CC0 |
| Rocket (`rocket.png`) | `skimage.data.rocket` | Public domain |
| House-head mascot walls / chimney | `skimage.data.brick` | CC0 |
| House-head roof | `skimage.data.gravel` | CC0 |
| Door-mouth frame 0 | `skimage.data.grass` texture + drawn | CC0 / original |

Everything else is original artwork drawn in `collage2/collage2.html`: the suit body, telephone handset, alarm clock,
scissors, hands, flowers, stars, RING!/POP! bursts, BOOKED cards, ransom lettering, the newsprint ground, the torn-paper
rip and all layout. The house-head mascot is an original character created for Elevate Home Ads (also in spot #1). The
cat-headed homeowner is an original collage character; no existing characters, brands or trademarks are used.

## Fonts (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1)
Anton (Vernon Adams) · Playfair Display (Claus Eggers Sørensen) · Special Elite (Astigmatic)

## Sound
All music and sound design synthesised in `collage2/make_audio.py` (numpy): boom-bap drums, Rhodes-style FM chords
(Cm9–F13), bass, vinyl crackle, telephone bells, snores, clock ticks, scissor snips, slaps, the comic pop, coin clinks,
whooshes and a paper rip. Voices: Kokoro-82M via kokoro-onnx (Apache-2.0): `af_bella` (the cat), `am_michael` (the
house), `af_heart` (end line).
