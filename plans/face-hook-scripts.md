# Face-hook scripts — batch 2

Short selfie clips of Oliver that open each animated ad. Say one line straight to the lens, then the animation takes over.
Each line runs about 2–4 seconds. Record 2–3 takes of every line you like; we A/B test the hooks against each other.

## How to record (applies to every hook)
- **Phone vertical, 4K if you have it, 30 fps.** Lens at eye level, arm's length or on a tripod. Keep your face in the
  upper-middle of the frame with a little headroom: that lets me crop the same clip for the 16:9 versions.
- **Light in front of you** (face a window, or stand outside in open shade). Not the sun or a window behind you.
- **Where:** truck, driveway, job site or office all work. Contractor-to-contractor reads better than a studio.
- **Start talking on the first frame.** No "hey guys", no intro, no smile-then-talk. Wait one beat of silence, say the
  line, then hold eye contact for one more beat before you stop recording. I trim both ends.
- **Sound:** a quiet spot, phone close (or a clip-on mic). Wind is the enemy.
- **Energy:** a bit punchier than normal conversation, like telling a buddy something he needs to hear. Don't shout.
- **Naming:** `hook-<video>-<letter>-take<n>`, e.g. `hook-05-B-take2.mov`. Upload them here and I'll cut them in.

## How a hook joins the animation
- **Replace:** your line *becomes* the video's first line. I cut the animation's opening voice line and the animation
  carries on from the next line. Total length stays the same.
- **Prepend:** your line plays first, then the full animation. Adds 2–4 s.
