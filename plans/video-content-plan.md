# Elevate Home Ads — video content plan

## Business profile (from the Q&A)
- **Who we serve:** home-improvement companies — roofing, remodeling, HVAC / plumbing / electrical, exterior & outdoor
  (windows, siding, decks, fencing, landscaping, pools, painting).
- **What we sell:** automated workflows built around each company's *actual* bottleneck — not "more leads" by default.
  If leads and booked jobs are fine but sales or marketing leaks, we automate that instead. Building blocks:
  booked-jobs system (speed-to-lead, qualifying, follow-up, tracking) · estimate follow-up · reviews & referrals ·
  old-lead / past-customer reactivation · sales-team automation (routing, tasks, missed-call text-back, reporting) ·
  insider-workflow websites (project planner → booked estimate) · ads management · monthly optimisation retainer.
- **Entry offer / CTA:** free **Booked-Jobs Leak Audit** — 5-question form → 20–30 min call mapping their funnel live →
  one-page leak report with the 2–3 workflows to build first.
- **Pricing stance:** performance-based ("you pay for results"). **Risk reverser:** no long contracts.
- **Proof available:** client testimonials; Oliver on camera (face hook + animation format).
- **Channels:** Meta / Instagram ads, organic social (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn), YouTube ads (bumpers + in-stream).
- **Tone:** straight-talking, contractor-to-contractor — premium and trustworthy, no hype, no jargon.

## Messaging pillars
1. **Leaks, not leads.** Most contractors don't need more leads; they need fewer leaks between lead and paid job.
2. **Built around your business.** We diagnose first, then automate *your* bottleneck — sales, follow-up, reviews, reactivation.
3. **Results, not retainers.** Performance-based, no long contracts.
4. **Runs without you.** Set up for you; your team doesn't learn new software.

## Format system
- **Face hook + animation:** Oliver records a 3–5 s phone selfie hook ("Contractors — you don't have a lead problem."),
  then the animation carries the explanation. Hooks are interchangeable across videos for A/B testing.
- Every video ships in **9:16** (Reels/Shorts/TikTok, Meta placements) and **16:9** (YouTube, LinkedIn); 6 s bumpers for YouTube.
- Every end card: **"Get your free Leak Audit"** · oliverelevatehomeads.com · "No long contracts. You pay for results."
- Captions burned in (most viewing is muted). Example numbers always labelled.

## Cadence
- 3–4 videos a week; each core video cut into 2–3 hook variants.
- Refresh paid creative every 60–90 days (fatigue); keep the best performers, replace the bottom half.
