# Course look: Online Reselling & Flipping

Keep every lesson in this course on this look, so the course reads as one series.

- **Style:** papercraft 3D diorama, using the engine from `papercraft/booked-jobs-papercraft.html`. Lesson 1 is
  `ascentra/reselling/m01-l01-progression.html`; copy it for the next lesson and replace the scenes.
- **Guide character:** the cardboard parcel (`parcel()` in lesson 1). It has kraft walls, top flaps that open when it
  cheers, ink eyes and a shipping label. It's an original character, so don't swap it for another.
- **Set:** a warm room with a wood floor and a wall of lit windows. Each scene stands on a round navy rug, with paper
  plants in the foreground.
- **Palette:**
  - kraft `#C89B62` and `#A97C45`
  - tape `#E6D3A6`
  - paper `#F3E9D4`
  - teal `#2E9C93`
  - coral `#E8705E`
  - navy `#1F3A5F`
  - butter `#F2D16B`
- **Type:**
  - Archivo 900 for the taped title strip
  - Oswald for labels and lower thirds
  - Fraunces 800 for the word-tag captions
- **Lesson tag:** "Online Reselling & Flipping · Module N · Lesson N", small, in the bottom-right corner.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `am_fenrir` at speed 0.92.
- **Music:** `scripts/make_music.py` at 88 BPM, transposed −2.
- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080.
- **Ending:** a recap scene with three numbered cards, then a "Quick check" sign with one question.
  Use "Next: …" when the brief gives a next lesson.
